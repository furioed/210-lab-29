My project will simulate the flow of a hospital and how each patient goes through each phase of the hospital over time. The purpose of this project is to improve flow and efficiency in hospitals by tracking the time it takes for a patient to enter and then leave the hospital at appropriate steps.

The simulation will be based around std::map, std::array, and std::list. std::map will store the department or area in the hospital the patient is in, like the ICU or surgery room, and std::array will store the three stages in our hospital. std::lists will represent the said three stages a patient typically goes through in the hospital: waiting, treatment, and discharge. The text file for this project will represent the patients, the phase they're in, the department they're in, and how much time they were in said department.

For the simulated events, it will track when a patient leaves a certain stage and goes to the next (i.e. from the waiting room to discharge). On top of this there will be delays or complications as well to simulate certain occurrences that happen in hospitals because these processes are not usually linear. Things like an emergency or max capacity will be accounted for. With this proposal, I think we can utilize this data to improve hospitals.
